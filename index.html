Customer-facing AI assistant

Why
AI answers are hard to rely on when no one can show how good they are or why they were given.

A conversational AI assistant used directly by end users, with a governance layer underneath it.

It defines what a good answer looks like, keeps its sources reliable, adds human review where automated checks fall short, and shows users why an answer was given.
